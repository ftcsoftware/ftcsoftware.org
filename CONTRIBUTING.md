# Contributing

Thank you for considering contributing to FTCSoftware.org!

FTCSoftware.org is a community driven project, and we rely on the work of volunteer contributors to write and maintain articles.

FTCSoftware.org is a fork of [FRCSoftware.org](https://github.com/frcsoftware/frcsoftware.org).
Since FTC and FRC share a control system starting with the 2027-2028 season, changes to site tooling and to content that applies to both programs are usually better contributed upstream, where they reach both audiences.
Contribute FTC-specific content here.

To learn more about contributing to the site and our styleguide, please visit our [contributing section](https://ftcsoftware.org/contribution/).
